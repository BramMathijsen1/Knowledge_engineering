REVIEW OF THE INSTRUMENT DATA (data/raw/instruments_raw.csv, data/curated/artist_instruments.csv)

Checked on 16 November 2026. The changes are on the branch TR_instruments_check.


WHAT WAS CHECKED
- Structure: every raw row has exactly one curated row, every kept instrument is in instruments.csv,
  every artist is in artists.csv, and every kept instrument is played by at least 3 of our artists.
- The same raw name is mapped the same way everywhere.
- The download was run again (16 November) and compared with the saved raw file.
- Every artist's list of instruments was read and compared with what is known about the artist.


OVERALL
The structure was correct and the download is reproducible: 1,509 of the 1,510 saved rows came back
unchanged. The only difference is Yes, which has 43 more rows in MusicBrainz now. But many bands were
missing their main members, and one mapping was wrong. Both are fixed on the branch.


PROBLEMS FOUND AND FIXED

1. Bands were missing their main members
   Problem:  MusicBrainz often gives instruments for only some members of a band, often later or minor
             ones. The Clash had only Nick Sheppard, a late guitarist. Tangerine Dream had no Edgar Froese
             and no synthesizer. Mahavishnu Orchestra had no John McLaughlin. Weather Report had no Joe
             Zawinul or Wayne Shorter. Run-DMC, N.W.A and Public Enemy had only their DJs, and no rappers.
             The Wikidata fallback only ran for bands with no MusicBrainz instruments at all, so one minor
             member was enough to block it. "eponymous" (a MusicBrainz word, not an instrument) also counted
             as an instrument, so the Sex Pistols and The Stooges got nothing at all.
   Fix:      fetch_instruments.py now takes Wikidata's instruments for every member that MusicBrainz lists
             with no instruments, as well as for bands with none at all.
   Result:   440 more raw rows. 122 more "artist plays instrument" facts, for 34 artists. 170 of our 181
             artists now have instruments (before: 168).
   Trade-off: Wikidata gives a member's instruments over their whole career, not only in that band. This
             is why MusicBrainz is still used first. The method page says so.

2. "membranophone" was mapped to percussion
   Problem:  MusicBrainz uses this word (the Hornbostel-Sachs class of drums) for some drummers: Earl Hudson
             and Mackie Jayson (Bad Brains), Jeff Nelson (Minor Threat), John Maher (Buzzcocks), Steve Jocz
             and Frank Zummo (Sum 41). Query 16 therefore said percussion was new in hardcore punk and
             pop-punk.
   Fix:      "membranophone" now maps to drum kit. The two false rows are gone from query 16.

3. New raw names
   Mapped by the existing rules: Gibson Les Paul, Gibson EDS-1275, classical guitar, steel guitar -> guitar;
   fretless bass -> bass guitar; Minimoog -> synthesizer; Roland TR-808 -> drum machine; Hammond organ,
   electronic organ -> organ; soprano saxophone -> saxophone; western concert flute -> flute;
   timbales -> percussion.
   Dropped as not in our list: alphorn, berimbau, laud, Suzuki Omnichord, "programming", "electrophone",
   "electronic musical instrument", "percussion guitare".

4. Banjo note
   The note said "Fewer than 3 of our artists play it". With Jerry Garcia (Grateful Dead) it is now 3, so
   the note now says "Not in our list of instruments".


EFFECT ON THE RESULTS

Query 17 (voice left out), artist-instrument pairs:
                      before        after
  both                277 (57%)     336 (58%)
  genre roots only    128 (27%)     161 (28%)
  neither              58 (12%)      58 (10%)
  influences only      20 (4%)       25 (4%)
The shares barely change, so the conclusion holds with more complete data.

Query 16: percussion in hardcore punk and pop-punk is gone. New: drum machine and piano in gangsta rap
(N.W.A and Dr. Dre), and drum kit, guitar and keyboards in ska. The ska rows come from the known problem
with solo singers in the parent genres (see the limits on the method page).

Queries 1 to 14 and 18 are unchanged. The ontology is unchanged.


POINTS NOT FIXED, TO DECIDE OR TO MENTION AS LIMITS

5. Solo artists depend on Wikidata, which has gaps: Johnny Cash has no guitar, Neil Young no voice,
   Marvin Gaye only voice. Fixing these by hand would make the data harder to rebuild, so they are a limit.

6. The Winstons still have no drum kit, because neither source has an instrument for their drummer
   G. C. Coleman (the Amen break).

7. Cello is played by 5 of our artists but is not in our list. The rule "at least 3 artists" is a minimum,
   not a reason to add an instrument, but this should be said, or cello should be added to Strings.

8. Query 17 compares pools of different sizes. "From influences" looks at the few artists who influenced
   this artist. "From genre roots" looks at every artist of every parent genre, which is a much bigger
   pool and so more likely to contain any instrument. Part of the gap between 28% and 4% comes from this,
   not from genres mattering more. A fairer test would compare with a random group of artists of the same
   size.

9. All rows are still marked "draft". The mapping of raw names to instruments (about 90 names) is the real
   decision. Once someone has read it, the rows can be marked "checked".
